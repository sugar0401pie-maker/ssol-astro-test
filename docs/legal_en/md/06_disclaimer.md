## Astrology is not science

Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a scientifically proven way to predict events or describe personality. Your reading interprets planetary positions at your birth. It does not tell you what will happen.

## How readings are made

Your results and star report are created automatically by our system, using rules and texts we wrote; for the paid star report, an AI model checks the tone and flow of the text. Sowella AI replies are written by AI. Automated and AI content can be wrong, incomplete or not right for you. Use your own judgment.

## Accuracy depends on your details

Your chart depends on the birth date, time and city you enter. If your birth time is unknown or approximate, parts of the reading (such as your Rising sign and houses) may not apply to you.

## If you are struggling

The Service is not for emergencies or crisis support. If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or feel unsafe, contact your local emergency number now. In the US or Canada, call or text 988. Elsewhere, find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

## How Sowella AI handles a crisis

Sowella AI is a wellness coaching chat for adults aged 18 and over. It may not be suitable for minors. Our safety protocol:

1. **You always know it's AI.** A notice that you are talking to AI, not a human, stays on the chat screen the whole time.
2. **We watch for signs of crisis.** Every message, in English and Korean, is checked with keyword rules and an AI review for signs of suicidal thoughts or self-harm.
3. **We point you to real help.** When a message shows those signs, Sowella AI stops its usual coaching and shows crisis contacts for your country, such as 988 in the US and Canada.
4. **We don't go into harmful detail.** Sowella AI is instructed never to give information about methods of self-harm or suicide.
5. **We review and improve.** We review how the protocol works and update it regularly with Korea-certified therapists and psychologists.
